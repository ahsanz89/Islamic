# DALIL privacy policy

The live, public version is published with the website at `https://ahsanz89.github.io/Islamic/privacy/` (source: `src/app/privacy/page.js`). That URL is the one to paste into App Store Connect and Google Play Console. If you change the policy, change it in that file so the app, stores and website never disagree.

Summary: no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no data sold or shared. Preferences, bookmarks and cached content stay on the device. Content is downloaded from ahsanz89.github.io; audio from everyayah.com, cdn.mp3quran.net and audio.qurancdn.com. No permissions requested.
