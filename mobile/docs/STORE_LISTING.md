## Long description (both stores, up to 4000)
DALIL (دليل) is a free, independent Islamic knowledge app. Explore. Learn. Understand.

CHOOSE YOUR TRANSLATION
The first time you open DALIL you pick the language you want to read alongside the Arabic. Change it any time from the button at the top of every reading screen or from Settings. The Arabic is always shown; the translation is yours to choose, and you can add a second one.

QURAN
• All 114 surahs in clear Quranic script
• Translations in English, Urdu, Hindi, Bengali, Indonesian, Turkish, French, Russian and Tamil
• Listen ayah by ayah with Mishary Alafasy and other reciters, or whole surahs with Badr Al-Turki
• Optional Urdu spoken translation after each ayah
• Adjustable Arabic text size, vowel marks on or off

HADITH
• Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim and more than twenty further collections, with chapters, grades and references
• Jump to any hadith number
• Hadith of the day

DUAS
• Authentic supplications, each linked to the hadith it comes from

MADE WITH CARE
• Light and dark appearance, English / Urdu / Arabic app language, right-to-left layout
• No accounts, no ads, no tracking
• Independent – not affiliated with any maslak, sect or organisation
