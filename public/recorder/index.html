DALIL Tutor Recorder

Thank you for helping! You will record short Arabic sounds so students hear a real teacher.
  1. Use a quiet room. Hold the phone or microphone close, about a hand-span from your mouth.
  2. Tap Record, say the Arabic shown clearly and calmly, tap Stop.
  3. Tap Play to check. Not happy? Record again.
  4. When finished (or any time you want to stop), tap Download recordings and send the file you get to the person who gave you this page.
Start with group 1 (letter names). The most important groups come first. Your work stays saved on this device if you close the page.
